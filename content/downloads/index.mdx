On this page you find ready-to-download PDFs of notarial documents selected from this database and gathered according to "document types" or "tags."

Refer to the sections below, or the menu on the right, to find out which document types are available at this time.

## Cameral obligation

Cameral obligation is a speficic form of payment that originated in the XIII century. To know more about how it is used in the notarial documents, please refer to the [essay by Antonia Fiori](https://nationalgalleryofart.github.io/accademiaessaysvolumeone/cameral_obligation/I_origin/), which has been published as part of this project.
The following PDF file includes more notarial documents that those cited in the essay by Fiori. The extract is based on the tag "Cameral Obligation" as appearing in the project's database.
You can find links to the notarial documents cited in the essay [here](https://nationalgalleryofart.github.io/accademiaessaysvolumeone/cameral_obligation/appendix/).
