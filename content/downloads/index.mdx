# Downloads

On this page you find downloadable PDFs created by the team of the *The Early History of the Accademia di San Luca* digital project at the Center for Advanced Study in the Visual Arts of the National Gallery of Art, Washington, D.C.

These PDFs contain notarial documents extracted from the database of our digital projects and gathered according to "document types" or "tags."

Please refer to the tiles below to find out which document types are available. Two different extracts might contain the same document: for instance, a "meeting" can also be included in "inventory" if the making of the inventory was called during a meeting.
 
Please cite the content of the PDF files according to the guidelines available [here](https://nationalgalleryofart.github.io/accademia-research/about/#how-to-cite).

Reproduction permissions for images or any other content in these PDF files must be secured by the user with the related archive, collection, or institution. No permissions for publication or reproduction are granted via the use of these PDF files. 

Please refrain from using the content of these documents to train AI models.

## Cameral obligation

Cameral obligation is a speficic form of payment that originated in the XIII century. To know more about how it is used in the notarial documents, please refer to the [essay by Antonia Fiori](https://nationalgalleryofart.github.io/accademiaessaysvolumeone/cameral_obligation/I_origin/), which has been published as part of this project.
The following PDF file includes more notarial documents that those cited in the essay by Fiori. The extract is based on the tag "Cameral Obligation" as appearing in the project's database.
You can find links to the notarial documents cited in the essay [here](https://nationalgalleryofart.github.io/accademiaessaysvolumeone/cameral_obligation/appendix/).
